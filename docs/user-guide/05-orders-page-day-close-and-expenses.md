# 5. Orders page, day close and expenses

## Orders – Orders ▾ → Orders

What it is for: all of today's orders in one list.

- Search by token or table; pages of 20.
- Each order shows status (Received, Preparing, Ready, Delivered, Completed, Cancelled), table/customer, items and total.
- Open an order to bill it, mark Food delivered / Handed over, cancel (before cooking), move, merge or split.

## Day close – Orders ▾ → Day close

What it is for: counting the till at the start and end of a shift so cash never goes missing.

1. **Open shift**: type the cash in the drawer (opening cash).
2. During the day: **Cash in / Cash out** for money added or taken (with reason). Expenses paid from the till also count as cash out.
3. **Close shift**: count the cash and type it. The screen shows **expected cash** (opening + cash sales + cash in − cash out) and the **difference**.
4. Print the **Z report** (day report) or tap **Share on WhatsApp** to send the summary to the owner or partner.

The day report shows: bills and sales, payments by Cash/UPI/Card (and small change waived), unpaid bills, GST summary, items sold, voids, expenses, tips per waiter, guest rating and **low stock** items.

## Expenses – Orders ▾ → Expenses

What it is for: money spent (milk, vegetables, gas, salary, rent…).

1. Choose a category, type the amount and a note.
2. **Paid from**: the till (comes out of the shift's expected cash) or elsewhere.
3. **Save expense**. Today's list and totals show below; reports include them.
