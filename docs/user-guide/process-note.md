# Process note

## What RestaurantPOS is

One app for the whole restaurant: the billing counter (POS), the kitchen screen, the token screen for guests, the menu, tables, stock, reservations, staff, bills with GST, day close and reports. It runs in any browser on a tablet, phone, laptop or PC. There are two editions:

- **Cloud**: the restaurant opens the website; nothing to install. Needs internet (it keeps taking orders for a while if the internet drops and sends them when it is back).
- **Shop PC**: the app runs on a small PC in the restaurant, so bills work without internet at all. A licence keeps it running (see page 10).

## Signing in

1. Open the restaurant's web address (for the test server: [https://13-236-102-22.sslip.io](https://13-236-102-22.sslip.io)).
2. Enter your email and password and tap **Sign in**.
3. You land on the right screen for your job: POS for cashier and waiter, Kitchen for kitchen staff, Platform for Mahesh.
4. Forgot the password? Tap **Forgot password?** – the owner gets a one-time code to set a new one. Staff ask the owner to reset theirs on the Staff page.

**New restaurant?** On the sign-in page tap **New restaurant? Start a 30-day free trial** (cloud only). Fill in the restaurant name, your name, email, mobile and password. The trial starts at once and you land on the setup checklist. **See prices and features** shows the plans.

## Languages

The buttons **EN · ગુ · हि** at the top right switch the screens between English, Gujarati and Hindi. Each person's choice is remembered on their own device. Printed bills and kitchen slips (KOT) have their own language setting (Admin ▾ → Billing).

## The main menu

The menu at the top is grouped so it fits on one line. Tap a group to open its list; the group of the page you are on is shown in green.

| Menu | Inside | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **POS** | – | Take orders, print KOT, make bills, take payment |
| **Orders ▾** | Orders, Reservations, Day close, Expenses | Today's orders and their status, table bookings, opening/closing the till, money spent |
| **Kitchen ▾** | Kitchen, Token screen | Kitchen tickets; big screen of ready tokens for guests |
| **Menu & stock ▾** | Menu, Stock, Tables | Dishes and prices, stock and recipes, tables and QR codes |
| **Reports ▾** | Reports, Feedback | Sales reports, GST and Tally exports, guest ratings |
| **Admin ▾** | Setup, Staff, Store, Settings, Billing, Licence | Owner settings |

You only see what your role is allowed to use. If a group has just one page for you, it shows as a plain link.

At the top right: your role, **?** (About and help), the language buttons and **Sign out**.

## Help inside the app

Tap **?** at the top right → **Help** for short how-to pages in English, Gujarati and Hindi, and the support phone/WhatsApp/email with support hours.

## The status bar

- A blue bar **"New version ready – reload"** means an update was installed; tap it when you are free.
- **"POS is restarting"** appears for a minute during an update on a shop PC; orders wait and are sent after.
- A yellow banner for the owner shows trial end, payment due or subscription stopped (cloud only).
