# 9. Guests: table QR ordering and feedback QR

## Table QR ordering

What it is for: guests order from their own phone; the table number is known automatically.

1. Print the QR sheet (Menu & stock ▾ → Tables → **Print QR sheet**) and stick each QR on its table.
2. The guest scans, sees the menu with search and category buttons, adds dishes and sends the order.
3. Depending on settings the order goes straight to the kitchen or waits for staff to confirm.
4. More orders from the same table are added to the table's open token. Guests also have **Call waiter** and **Request bill** buttons.
5. Once the bill is printed, the QR asks guests to call staff to add more.

Each QR contains a secret code (not just the table number). If a code leaks, regenerate it on the Tables page.
