# 6. Stock and recipes

## Stock – Menu & stock ▾ → Stock

What it is for: knowing how much milk, paneer, oil, cups… you have, and getting warned before it runs out.

### Add a stock item (owner/manager)

1. Tap **Add stock item**.
2. Name (e.g. Milk), unit (kg, g, l, ml, pcs, pack, box, dozen), quantity in stock now, and **Warn when at or below** (low level, e.g. 5 l).
3. Save.

### Record what happened (cashier, kitchen, manager)

Tap **Record** on an item and choose:

- **Bought** – quantity, total paid (optional) and **supplier** (optional). The cost per unit is worked out from it.
- **Used** – used by hand (recipes do this automatically).
- **Wasted** – spoilt or thrown away, with a note.
- **Counted (sets the stock)** – after a physical count, type what you actually have; the difference is recorded.
Stock never goes below zero by hand entry.

### See low stock and history

- Items at or under their low level show a yellow **Low** mark and a message at the top. **Only low stock** filters the list.
- **History** shows every change: when, what, change, quantity after, note (and token number for recipe use).

## Recipes – Menu & stock ▾ → Menu → Recipe

What it is for: stock goes down by itself when you sell.

1. On the Menu page tap **Recipe** next to a dish.
2. Add stock items and how much **one portion** uses, e.g. Masala Chai: Milk 0.15 l, Sugar 0.01 kg.
3. **Save recipe**.
When an order is completed (paid and served), its portions are taken from stock as **Used** with the token number. Sales are never blocked: stock can show below zero until your next count.

## Stock report – Stock → Stock report (owner/manager)

What it is for: what you spent, used and wasted, and what your shelf is worth.

- Choose dates (last 7 days by default) and tap **Show**.
- Totals: **Bought for**, **Wasted worth**, **Stock value now** (using each item's last purchase price).
- Per item: bought, cost, used, wasted, in stock, value.
- Per supplier: number of purchases and amount.

## Low stock on the day report

The day report lists low items and adds them to the WhatsApp summary, so the owner sees what to buy tomorrow.
