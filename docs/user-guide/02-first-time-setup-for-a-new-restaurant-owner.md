# 2. First-time setup for a new restaurant (owner)

Do these once, in this order. **Admin ▾ → Setup** shows a checklist that ticks itself as you finish each step.

> 📷 Screenshot: Setup checklist
> 

## 1. Store details – Admin ▾ → Store

What it is for: everything printed at the top and bottom of the bill.

1. Enter the restaurant and outlet name, address and phone.
2. Enter your **GSTIN** (needed for GST bills) and **FSSAI** number.
3. Upload your **logo** (it prints on bills and shows on screens).
4. Write the **receipt footer**, e.g. "Thank you, visit again".
5. Tap **Save**.

## 2. Billing – Admin ▾ → Billing

What it is for: how bills are calculated and printed.

- **Tax mode**: Regular GST (CGST + SGST on each item), Composition (Bill of Supply, no tax shown) or No GST.
- **Prices include GST**: tick if your menu prices already include tax.
- **Bill prefix**: 1–3 letters, e.g. INV → bills are INV/26-27/000001. Numbers never skip or repeat.
- **UPI ID and name**: e.g. yourshop@okaxis. The bill then shows a UPI QR with the exact amount.
- **Paper width**: 80 mm or 58 mm thermal paper.
- **Bill and KOT language**: English, Gujarati or Hindi.
- **Small change a cashier may waive (₹)**: 0–20, default 5 (see page 3).

## 3. Settings – Admin ▾ → Settings

What it is for: how your outlet works.

- **Business type preset**: dine-in restaurant, quick service/takeaway counter, cafe, cloud kitchen, stall or canteen. Choosing one fills sensible settings; you can still change each one.
- **Order types**: dine-in, takeaway or both; which one is the default.
- **Customer name / phone** for takeaway: off, optional or required.
- **Kitchen screen** on or off. If off, a paid order closes at once.
- **Tokens**: first token number of the day.
- **Quantity limit** per line (stops typing 100 by mistake).
- **Approvals**: the biggest discount a cashier may give without a manager PIN; whether a bill reprint needs a PIN.
- **Feedback QR on bill** and your **Google review link**.

## 4. Tables – Menu & stock ▾ → Tables

What it is for: the tables guests sit at (new restaurants start with 10).

1. **Add table**, give it a number or name (e.g. 5, Garden 2).
2. A table that was never used can be **deleted**; a used one can be set **Not in use**.
3. **Print QR sheet**: one QR per table for guests to order from their phone (page 9).

## 5. Menu – Menu & stock ▾ → Menu

What it is for: the dishes, prices and categories the POS shows.

- **Fastest way: menu from a photo.** Tap **Scan menu**, take or upload a photo of your paper menu. The app reads the dishes and prices into a table. Check and correct them, then save. Nothing is saved until you confirm.
- **Categories**: add (e.g. Starters, Thali, Drinks). A category can have serving hours (e.g. Breakfast 7–11 am); outside them its dishes are hidden on the POS.
- **Items**: name, price, category, GST rate (usually 5%), veg/non-veg, description, kitchen station. The **Available** switch hides an item that is sold out today.
- **Sold by**: Piece (normal), **Per kg** or **Per litre**. For paneer, sweets, farsan or chaas, set the price per kg or litre; the POS then asks how much and works out the line price.
- **Sizes & extras**: e.g. Pizza Regular/Large, extra cheese +₹30.
- **Combo**: a set meal made of other items.
- **Recipe**: what stock one portion uses (page 6).
- **Kitchen stations**: e.g. Tandoor, Chinese – each prints its own KOT.
- Use the search box and category filter to find items quickly.

## Quick setups for counters

On **Admin ▾ → Settings**, two more quick setups:

- **Bakery / sweet shop**: dine-in or takeaway, no table, no customer name. The bill opens right after the order.
- **Dairy shop**: takeaway only, same simple counter. Use **Sold by: Per kg / Per litre** on items such as paneer, dahi or chaas.

Both turn on **Simple counter** (no table or customer name, the bill opens right after the order). You can turn it on or off by hand too.

## Sign-in security – Admin ▾ → Security

- **Authenticator app** (owner and manager): after the password, a 6-digit code from Google Authenticator or a similar app. Works offline. You get 10 one-time backup codes: print them and keep them away from your phone.
- **Change password**: type the current password, then the new one twice. Your other devices are signed out; this one stays signed in.

## 6. Staff – Admin ▾ → Staff

What it is for: a login for every person, so bills and changes show who did them.

1. **Add staff**: name, email (their sign-in), role (Manager, Cashier, Waiter, Kitchen) and a password.
2. **Set PIN** for managers: a 4–6 digit PIN used to approve big discounts, voids and reprints on a cashier's screen.
3. **Reset password** when someone forgets theirs. **Disable** someone who has left (their old bills keep their name).
4. A **kitchen screen device** can stay signed in for a long time.

## 7. Printer

Bills and KOTs print through the browser's print window. Set the thermal printer as default and paper size to 80 mm (or 58 mm). Do one test print from the POS before opening.

## 8. Try it

Place a test order, make a bill, pay it, then void it (reason "test"). You are ready.
