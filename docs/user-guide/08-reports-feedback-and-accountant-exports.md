# 8. Reports, feedback and accountant exports

## Reports – Reports ▾ → Reports (owner/manager)

What it is for: how the business is doing.

1. Choose **From / To** or tap **Today**, **Last 7 days**, **This month**. Today follows the business day (starts 4 AM).
2. You see: bills, sales, discounts, GST, net sales, sales by day, item, category, payment method, hour and staff, expenses, and guest rating.
3. Downloads for your accountant:
    - **Download GST bills (CSV)** – every bill with GST split, for returns.
    - **Download sales (CSV)** – sales lines for Excel.
    - **Download for Tally (XML)** – import into Tally: one sales voucher per day with Cash, UPI, Card, CGST, SGST and round off ledgers; credit notes too.

## Day report (Z report)

From Day close or Reports: the end-of-day summary as it prints, with **Print** and **Share on WhatsApp**.

## Feedback – Reports ▾ → Feedback

What it is for: what guests think.

- If **Feedback QR on bill** is on, every bill has a QR. Guests scan it and give 1–5 stars and a comment.
- The Feedback page lists them with filters by stars; the average shows on reports and the day summary.
- Happy guests (4–5 stars) are shown your **Google review** link if you set one.

## Credit notes

If a paid bill must be cancelled (e.g. refund), a manager makes a **credit note** with a reason and refund method. The original bill stays; the credit note cancels it in reports and Tally.

## Activity log

**Admin → Activity log** (owner only; managers, cashiers, waiters and kitchen staff do not see it) lists every change, newest first: **when**, **who** (the staff email, or *system*), **what** (for example *Changed · Menu item #5*) and the **details** (for example *Price: 18 → 20*). Use **Type** to show one kind of change only (menu items, tables, staff, cancellations, support ...). Rows of a **support session** are highlighted in yellow with a *Support* badge, so you always see what support did in your restaurant. The page shows the latest 500 changes, 20 per page.
